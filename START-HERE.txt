RUNICWORLD PROGRESSION PATHWAY

INSTALL ON YOUR EXISTING WEBSITE
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload its files directly to the ROOT of davidja92/runicworld-quest-hub.
   Replace matching files. Do not put them in a new subfolder.
3. Keep your existing GitHub workflows and other repository files.
4. Wait for GitHub Pages to deploy, then refresh your site with Ctrl+F5.
5. Click the gold "Progression pathway" button at the top.

USE IT
Fetch your character's skills and enter your current quest points.
Make sure your completed quests are marked correctly.
Choose a goal, then click "Resume next step".

Goals:
- Quests & useful unlocks: available quest requirements plus practical milestones.
- Available quest catalogue: work through your site's available quests.
- Quests + Fletching 80: keep questing while building your bow-making chain.
- Quests + all skills 70 or 99: continue skill targets after quest requirements.
- Add your own skill targets under Preferences.

Quest steps open your existing full quest helper.
Preparation steps list a starter quest kit; check the full guide for alternatives.
Skill steps show client-listed methods, XP remaining and action estimates.
Choose a method or save your observed XP if RunicWorld differs from the client.
Refresh skills after training AND quest rewards so the plan can shorten the grind.
Your actual progress is never advanced just because the plan predicts a step.

The rolling bank checklist combines the next three quest kits.
Enter your actual bank/inventory counts; update them after spending materials.
Reusable tools are reserved once, consumables are added across those quests.
Some items, quantities and alternatives require a full guide check.
"Kit prepared" saves that preparation step; it does not complete the quest.

Self-sourcing and no minigames are the defaults.
Fletching prefers yew longbows unstrung when your actual level allows them.
Woodcutting prefers yew logs. You can change methods in a skill step.
"Find source" shows production options, matched shop references and a Wiki link.
Enable "Show GE links" if you want the existing market tools instead.

The quest-start map uses your client's coordinates. Its lines show step order,
not a safe walking route or obstacle-aware navigation.

BACKUP
Export/import saves pathway preferences, bank counts, observed XP and kit checks.
It does not overwrite quest completions, hiscores or quest-point balances.
Pathway settings are saved separately per character in your browser.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- All 130 entries from your quest catalogue.
- 127 Wiki requirement references; 3 RunicWorld-specific entries need checking.
- 123 client quest-start locations.
- 423 simple item-reference entries for quest starter kits.
- Your existing RunicWorld recipes, GE tracker, Kudos and total-level card.

ABOUT "OPTIMAL"
The route favors quests before avoidable grinding, useful unlocks, prerequisite
chains and smaller training gaps. It uses quest XP as an OSRS reference to rank
opportunities, not as confirmed future RunicWorld XP. Same-map start proximity
breaks later ties. No minimum-time or maximum-profit guarantee is made without
measured action rates, travel data and server reward verification.

Modern OSRS quests absent from your catalogue are not added.
Minigame methods are hidden by default. Server-specific unlocks, combat,
equipment, partial quest starts, farming timers and item sources need checking.
Skill targets use unboosted levels; guides may allow boosts or bypasses.
